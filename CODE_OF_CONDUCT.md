# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, the maintainer and contributors of STMSharp commit to making participation in the project a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to be respectful in discussion, to accept constructive criticism gracefully, and to focus on what is best for the project and its users. Technical disagreement is welcome and is a normal part of building a concurrency library; it should be conducted on the merits and without personal hostility.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment of any kind, personal or political attacks, publishing the private information of others without permission, and any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including the issue tracker, pull requests, and any other forum used by the project, and it applies when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the maintainer at francesco.delre[at]protonmail.com. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. The maintainer is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.
